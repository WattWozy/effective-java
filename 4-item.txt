-> what is a utility class? Also known as helper class, a utility class’ goal 
is to provide with stateless, reusable methods. The conventions to defining a 
utility class are: define the class as final, keep the constructor private (noninstantiable)
and make all its methods static.

A class is made non-instantiable by making its constructor private.
A constructor is always generated for a class if not explicitly defined. Thus, defining one and
making it private is the way. 
Abstract is not helpful: it can be inherited and used through a subclass.
Subclasses invoke explicitly or implicitly their parent’s constructor.
